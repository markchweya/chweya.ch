## What Dumi is

Dumi is an unofficial AI assistant that answers questions about information
published by the Canton of Zug. It is not operated or endorsed by the canton.

## What we do with your question

Your question is used to search official Canton of Zug pages and to produce an
answer. It is **not stored**. When you close the page, it is gone.

Please do not enter personal, confidential, medical, legal, tax or
case-specific information. Dumi cannot look up your file and has no access to
any personal record.

## What we do keep

- A one-way, keyed hash of your network address, so that one user cannot
  overload the service for everyone. It cannot be reversed to your address
  without a key held separately from the database.
- Technical logs about whether the service was working. These do not contain
  your question.

## What we do not do

- We do not create an account for you or track you across visits.
- We do not use your question to train any model.
- We do not send your question to a third-party AI service.

## Cookies

The public assistant sets no cookies. Staff who sign in to the administration
area receive one session cookie, which is deleted when they sign out.

## Your rights

Because we do not store your questions and do not identify you, there is
normally no personal data of yours to access, correct or delete.
