Dumi Identity · draft 01

Dumi

Three lenses of light circulating around a still core: the shape of an assistant looking something up for you.

The idea

Light circulating around a fixed centre

Alva is a soap bubble: iridescent, soft, undirected. Dumi has a centre and things move around it. That difference is the whole mark.

The core stays still

Every orbiting light returns to the same fixed point. The job here is retrieving one right answer out of canton records, and a mark organised around a centre carries that better than a swirl.

One warm spark

Amber, small and tight, orbiting fastest. It keeps a deep blue institutional orb from reading cold. It is also the single token a canton recolours to make the mark its own.

The mark is the status

This same element is the launcher, the message avatar and the thinking indicator, so its motion has a job. Tempo and core brightness report what Dumi is doing. There is no separate spinner anywhere in the product, and no typing dots.

Scale

Legible from 24 px to hero

Blur, rim and inner shadow are all proportional to --dumi-size, so the mark still reads as a sphere across the whole range. 24 px is the message avatar, 64 px the launcher.

States

What the motion reports

One unitless multiplier, --dumi-tempo, drives all three orbits. Under prefers-reduced-motion they freeze at composed angles, and state falls back to halo and core brightness.

Idle

Present and waiting

A 13-second drift you notice only if you look. This is the launcher sitting in the corner of a canton page.

Listening

Taking your question

The halo blooms outward while the orbits hold their pace. Nothing is being computed yet, and the mark should not pretend otherwise.

Thinking

Searching the records

Every orbit accelerates roughly fourfold and the core lights up. This is what replaces the spinner.

In place

Zug, doing its job

The canton owns the page; Dumi is the help. Same split Alva uses, and the one residents already read correctly.

Palette

Glacial, with one warm spark

Swiss water and ice. Each value is stored as an RGB triplet so it can take an alpha anywhere it is used.

Glacier — orb body #0B3B57 --dumi-deep
Flow — primary light #1E88A8 --dumi-flow
Ice — fast highlight #6FD6D2 --dumi-ice
Spark — canton slot #E8A33D --dumi-accent
Canton system

One token per canton

Geometry, tempo, palette and motion are shared. A canton folder overrides --dumi-accent-rgb and nothing else, which keeps Dumi recognisably one assistant across all 26.

Favicon

Sixteen pixels, still Dumi

Shrinking the mark does not work. At icon size the soft orbit collapses into a dark blob, so the favicon is a separate static drawing: one composed frame of the orbit, plus a second cut for 16 px that lifts the body, enlarges the core and drops the rim. Everything below is the real file, at the size a browser will use it.
