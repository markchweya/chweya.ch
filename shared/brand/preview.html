Dumi Identity · draft 01

Dumi

Three lenses of light circulating around a still core — the assistant looking something up on your behalf.

The idea

Circulation, not shimmer

Alva is a soap bubble — iridescent, soft, undirected. Dumi has a centre and things move around it. That difference is the whole mark.

The core stays still

Every orbiting light returns to the same fixed point. For an assistant whose one job is retrieving the right answer from canton records, a mark organised around a centre says more than one that swirls.

One warm spark

Amber, small and tight, orbiting fastest. It keeps a deep-blue institutional orb from reading cold — and it is the single token a canton recolours to make the mark its own.

The mark is the status

This same element is the launcher, the message avatar and the thinking indicator. So the motion is functional: tempo and core brightness report what Dumi is doing, with no separate spinner.

Scale

Legible from 24 px to hero

The blur, rim and inner shadow are all proportional to --dumi-size, so the sphere reading survives the whole range. 24 px is the message avatar, 64 px the launcher.

States

What the motion reports

One unitless multiplier, --dumi-tempo, drives all three orbits. Under prefers-reduced-motion the orbits freeze at composed angles and state falls back to halo and core brightness alone.

Idle

Present, not working

A 13-second drift you notice only if you look. This is the launcher sitting in the corner of a canton page.

Listening

Taking your question

The halo blooms outward while the orbits hold their pace. Growth, not speed — nothing is being computed yet.

Thinking

Searching the records

Every orbit accelerates roughly fourfold and the core lights up. Replaces the spinner entirely.

In place

Zug, doing its job

The canton owns the page; Dumi is the help. Same split Alva uses, and the one residents already read correctly.

Palette

Glacial, with one warm spark

Swiss water and ice, not the pastel iridescence every AI assistant reaches for. Stored as RGB triplets so any value takes an alpha.

Glacier — orb body #0B3B57 --dumi-deep
Flow — primary light #1E88A8 --dumi-flow
Ice — fast highlight #6FD6D2 --dumi-ice
Spark — canton slot #E8A33D --dumi-accent
Canton system

One token per canton

Everything else — geometry, tempo, palette, motion — is shared. A canton folder overrides --dumi-accent-rgb and nothing else, so Dumi stays recognisably one assistant across all 26.
